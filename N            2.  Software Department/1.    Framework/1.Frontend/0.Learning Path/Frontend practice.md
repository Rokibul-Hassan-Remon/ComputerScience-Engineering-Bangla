## 🎯 **Frontend Interview Practice & Coding Challenges (Free / Community Based)**

### 🧠 1. **BFE.dev (BigFrontEnd.dev)**

- A _LeetCode‑like_ platform focused on frontend interview prep.
    
- Huge question bank including **JavaScript, React, TypeScript, CSS, and system design** questions.
    
- In‑browser editor with submissions + community solutions.
    
- Free to use with community features. [BigFrontend](https://bigfrontend.dev/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    

👉 Great for structured interview practice with frontend‑focused coding questions.

---

### 📚 2. **Hack Frontend**

- Free frontend interview prep site with **real interview questions** and explanations.
    
- Covers **JavaScript, React, HTML/CSS** and more, plus live coding right on the site.
    
- Built for practice + theory. [Hack Frontend+1](https://www.hackfrontend.com/en?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    

👉 Good foundation before jumping into tougher interview rounds.

---

### 🧩 3. **Frontend Challenges (Open Source)**

- _Open source_ challenge collection with problems in **JavaScript, CSS, React, etc.**
    
- Community‑run and you can even contribute.
    
- Has leaderboard and GitHub repo you can explore or fork. [Frontend Challenges](https://frontend-challenges.com/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    

👉 Ideal if you want _open community contributions_ and free interview‑style problems.

---

### 💻 4. **Frontend Mini Challenges (GitHub)**

- A **GitHub open repo** full of frontend challenges for practice + interviews.
    
- Supports **vanilla JS**, React, Vue, Angular with solutions included. [GitHub](https://github.com/sadanandpai/frontend-mini-challenges?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    

👉 You can **clone, practice locally, improve, or even extend it yourself**.

---

### ❓ 5. **100 Days of Frontend Interview Questions (GitHub)**

- A community repo with **500+ real interview questions** spanning HTML, CSS, JS, React, TS.
    
- Free and great for pencil‑and‑paper or flashcard‑style revision. [GitHub](https://github.com/Saran-pariyar/100_Days_Of_Frontend_Interview_Questions?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    

👉 Good for daily structured interview prep.

---

### 🧪 6. **GreatFrontend.com**

- Massive bank of **500+ frontend interview problems** with explanations and in‑browser coding/tracks.
    
- Focuses on real‑world and interview‑style tasks (including UX/UI, JS logic, React). [GreatFrontEnd](https://www.greatfrontend.com/questions?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    

👉 Excellent alternative to DevTools if you want _frontend + coding_ questions.

---

## 🛠️ Other Useful Tools for Practice (not strictly “interview question banks,” but helpful)

- Free, open‑source coding practice in many languages including JavaScript.
    
- Community feedback and mentorship for exercises. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exercism?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    

👉 Best for sharpening _core language skills_ which is vital in interviews.
